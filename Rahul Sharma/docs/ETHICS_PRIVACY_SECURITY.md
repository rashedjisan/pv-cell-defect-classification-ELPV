# Ethics, Privacy and Security

- Public research image datasets are used; no personal information is intentionally collected.
- Source provenance and licensing are documented under `data/` and `artifacts/documentation/`.
- Raw third-party datasets are not redistributed in this repository.
- The interface is a proof-of-concept research tool. It does not provide professional photovoltaic inspection, maintenance or safety advice.
- Model outputs should be interpreted with calibration, robustness, subgroup/domain-shift evidence and known limitations.
- Production deployment would require authentication, encrypted transport, least-privilege storage, audit logging, monitoring and controlled model promotion; these controls are outside the implemented research scope.
